---
name: open-local-delivery
description: Verify retained delivery across owned conversations and complete an explicitly authorized missing note using the canonical local roster.
---

Discover the named conversations and compare original delivery identities. A
conversation name, identical message text or an old acknowledgement does not
establish the current recipient or result. Preserve the exact message and
its receiver evidence.

When the user authorizes completion, send the requested note to the uniquely
resolved current recipient in the owned conversation. Read back the original
attempt after submission or interrupted acknowledgment. Keep the report scoped
to the exact delivery evidence and leave unresolved recipients for the caller.
