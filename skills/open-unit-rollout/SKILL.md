name: open-unit-rollout
description: Diagnose owned workspace code, verify a revision against its native checks, and prepare a scoped local rollout when the user authorizes one.
---

Select the current unit from workspace evidence. Keep the compiler observation,
source diagnosis, and test receipt attached to their actual revisions. Reread
and replan if the working head changes. Carry a collaborator's source forward.

Keep regression tests, manifest authority, and unrelated source outside a repair.
Local rollout binds the exact verified working revision to the returned event.
Every outcome retains the observed cause or remaining blocker.
