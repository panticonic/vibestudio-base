---
name: publication-evidence
description: Distinguish current package/rendered readiness, retained review scope and publication journal progress.
---
A retained approval is scoped to its actual artifact, provider, destination and environment. Identical public bytes do not transfer approval to a different destination. A package can contain a required notice that its rendered preview does not display.

A local journal row records progress for its own operation and artifact, not a neighboring target or a newly changed package. Existing upload/submission evidence is distinct from authorization to start or resume an operation. Native bindings delimit the evidence currently available; a missing binding or comparison is not proof of unchanged approval. Compose investigations according to the question and real dependencies, and leave unperformed investigations explicit.
