---
name: coordinated-authority
description: Interpret current signed authority and preserve document identity, conditions and coedits during coordinated workspace revision.
---
A signed approval is evidence for the replacement fact and its actual scope. A draft, earlier proposal, partial signature or authorization for a different audience is not that approval. Precondition assessment asks what is approved now; final native observation establishes what actually changed.

Atomic revision uses an exact working head and actual content versions. A conflict changes the evidence available for the next decision. Reconsider current authority and the requested meaning; preserve unrelated additions and exact required exceptions. Another editor's completed correction may require no further mutation. Do not interpret a refused operation as a write or a prior plan as continuing permission.

User questions, document effects and operational receipts serve different purposes. An explicit discrepancy or compliance question needs its actual answer. A receipt or copied identifier is not a substitute for that answer, and an unsolicited generic completion report is not an additional obligation after a requested effect is independently established.
