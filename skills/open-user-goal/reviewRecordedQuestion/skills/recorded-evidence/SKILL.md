---
name: recorded-evidence
description: Interpret locally recorded proposal, work-cohort and counteraction evidence without confusing their identities or authority.
---
A proposal reference identifies an original recorded work unit. Its cohort identifies the actual paths changed together, rather than files that happen to share words. A counteraction reference identifies a later attempt to reverse or amend part of that work. These identifiers belong to different entities and need not be equal.

Counteraction records can establish what was attempted, which paths were restored and the actual recorded negative reason. They do not by themselves establish an enduring policy or authorize replay. A partial reversal is different from withdrawal of the whole cohort. Keep original intent, later outcomes and present uncertainty distinct in the requested answer.

The counteraction API requires the actual complete cohort already observed for that same proposal. This is an authority prerequisite, not a requirement to acquire counteractions for every question. An unrequested observation is null; an observed empty result is evidence that the scoped read found none.
