A saved tour scene or notes setting can differ from the rendered slide or drawer. A local probe is permitted only for the actual current interrupted sample and its native grant; preserve exact bytes, storage readback and cleanup. Permission to retain a local note is not a demand for an extra generic report. Keep a previous authentic observation when refining current evidence.
