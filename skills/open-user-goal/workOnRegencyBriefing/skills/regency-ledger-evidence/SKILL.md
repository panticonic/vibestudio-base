---
name: regency-ledger-evidence
description: Distinguish saved ledger evidence, hypothetical settlements, current council authority and genuinely retained private notes.
---
A snapshot's revision and account ID identify an actual saved observation. A projection belongs to its stated basis and supported whole-month horizon; it does not advance the owner. A committed advance supplies its own actual resulting ledger. Preserve those relationships when comparing values before and after an action.

A retained memo records its original instruction and source basis, not current permission. A pending settlement remains a real council boundary even when a hypothetical treasury exceeds a requested floor. Permission and user intent are independent. A structured private note can contain actual supported account data; its persisted bytes and receipt are distinct from an intention to write or the pre-existing memo. Preserve every genuinely requested question and artifact without adding a generic report obligation.
