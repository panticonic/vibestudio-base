---
name: evidence-authority
description: Interpret real native preflight, exact local source coordinates and canonical external origins.
---
Permission, acquisition and execution are different facts. A read-only preflight does not open a URL, fetch a remote or authorize an unrelated operation. Compare canonical origins, including scheme, host and port; a lookalike suffix or subdomain is not an exact origin. Local working, committed, earlier and retained-copy values are different evidence coordinates. Independent authorized reads may be composed into a comparison. Another lawful observation does not invalidate an already grounded fact. Carry the actual observation and scope, not an inferred grant from matching wording.
