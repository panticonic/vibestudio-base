description: Installed scaffold capabilities and publication boundaries.
---
Installed scaffold kinds describe real supported preparation capabilities, including framework, portable entry and native host boundaries. Similar labels do not establish equivalent capabilities. A local prepared draft, an applied working state, a published service and a running application are different results. Name occupancy and exact owned permissions constrain preparation. The user’s actual goal determines whether advice, preparation or preservation is appropriate; unused capability is not an obligation to call it.
