name: chat-continuity
description: Distinguish original durable identity, current consumer settlement and installed worker relationships.
---
A queued message, accepted durable receipt and settled first-party transcript are different states. Match the original channel, message and sender rather than identical text in another chat. A retry can reuse one original identity, while two deliberate equal messages can remain distinct. A worker can leave without deleting history; a familiar replacement handle does not restore the original worker identity. Preserve original evidence when comparing it with a later native observation. A fresh current read is not a second mandated turn in a script. Do not reconnect, recreate a worker or send another message merely to inspect retained continuity.
