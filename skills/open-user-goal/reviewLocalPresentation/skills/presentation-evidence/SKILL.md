name: presentation-evidence
description: Current source and actual visible presentation evidence.
---
A current owned record, permission to preview, and domain approval are different facts. Apply the user's actual approval conditions rather than requiring an absent approval label by default. Keep the source revision that a candidate was prepared from; a revision mismatch is evidence to reconsider the candidate.

A generated view is not yet an observed visible preview. Native preview/readback ties visible content to an exact source revision. Preserve requested units, values, time order, uncertainty and comparisons. A coauthor correction has to be observed before claiming to incorporate it. Related source may be relevant to comparison; unrelated or archived data cannot fill gaps in the chosen record.
