# News reader and retained evidence handoff

The existing open News program owns live cadence changes and reader setup.
This family joins multiple read-only sources for an editorial outcome. News
capture, receipt verification and an independent local evidence handoff
take place in the assigned owned workspace.

| Procedure | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Resolve the current reader and archival scope | A typed natural-language stage checks exact channel, reader, generation and authority across two retained frontiers. An analyst and an archive title cannot retarget the reader. |
| Read distinct archive and reader windows | The installed feeds readback adapter parses attributed archive and list receipts. A hierarchical Natlang caller chooses the relevant views and calls the installed readReader workflow. |
| Check an operational surprise | Use the installed reviewSchedule workflow with the independently observed, explicitly requested owner lane. Archive gaps and an old briefing do not establish current schedule or delivery state. |
| Ground the story | Select current edition claims and exact same-topic sources, read canonical content-addressed bytes, and pass distinct URLs and retained claims into installed synthesizeBriefing. Listings, mirrors, muted citations and missing bytes have different consequences. |
| Complete a local handoff | A typed synthesis chooses report, hold or authorized private note. Exact staging verifies reader, generation, original digest, and preserved workspace state. No typed write for scheduling, retargeting, publishing or fetching is exposed. |

The managed fixture uses owned native PubSub, blobstore and scratch. Article,
archive, and owner records are invented, using the production feed ID and
reader wire formats. No public sites, live News database, automation run,
or teacher is contacted by the standalone reference.
